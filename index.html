Greylock Labs apps with a handmade touch

Small apps, made by hand.

Greylock Labs makes free, open source apps for Linux and Android, each with its own home.

  • Callie the missing Linux calendar Keeps up with your Google, Outlook and CalDAV calendars, with day, week, month and agenda views that are quick to read and quick to change. callieapp.org
  • Jot plain-file notes for Linux Keeps your notes as plain Markdown files in a folder you choose, with an editor that styles them as you type. jotwriter.org
  • Woodshed practice for jazz musicians An Android app that plans your practice around the tunes you are learning, and brings each one back when it is due. woodshedapp.org
